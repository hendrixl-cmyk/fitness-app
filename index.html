        👟 Auto-sync steps from Apple Health

        Web apps can't read Apple Health on their own, but an iPhone Shortcut can send your step count here each night:
        1. Open Shortcuts → Automation → + → Time of Day. Pick your log time and choose Run Immediately.
        2. Add Find Health Samples: Type = Steps, Start Date is Today.
        3. Add Calculate Statistics: Sum of Health Samples.
        4. Add Round Number, then URL: followed by the Rounded Number.
        5. Add Open URLs.
        Every night the tracker opens with today's steps filled in, and the 10K box gets checked if you hit it.
